Comparing Strategy
Don't compute both sides! Instead, look at what changed between the two expressions. If one term got bigger and nothing else changed, the whole expression got bigger.

Key Concept
To compare expressions without computing: identify which terms changed and by how much. If one side gained more than it lost compared to the other, it's bigger. Use properties (commutative, distributive) to recognize when expressions are equal.
