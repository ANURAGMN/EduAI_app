How Zooming Works
To locate 4.185: first find it's between 4 and 5. Zoom in — it's between 4.1 and 4.2. Zoom again — between 4.18 and 4.19. One more — exactly at 4.185. Each zoom splits a section into 10 equal parts!

Key Concept
Each decimal place is 10× more precise. Tenths split a unit into 10 parts. Hundredths split a tenth into 10 parts (100 per unit). Thousandths split further (1000 per unit). Trailing zeros (0.20 = 0.2) don't change the value!
