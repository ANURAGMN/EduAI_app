Indian vs International

See how the same number looks in two systems

Comma Placement Rules
Indian: 3-2-2-2 pattern from right (Thousands, Lakhs, Crores, Arabs)
International: 3-3-3 pattern from right (Thousands, Millions, Billions)
Indian= International
1 Lakh (1,00,000)100 Thousand
10 Lakhs (10,00,000)1 Million
1 Crore (1,00,00,000)10 Million
1 Arab (1,00,00,00,000)1 Billion

How to Use This Simulation

What To Do
1In Explore mode, drag the slider to change the number. Watch how the same number looks different in Indian and International systems.
2Notice the coloured commas — red commas (Indian) are placed in a 3-2-2-2 pattern, blue commas (International) in a 3-3-3 pattern.
3Read the word form below each display. "Lakh" and "Crore" are Indian terms; "Million" and "Billion" are International.
4Switch to Quiz mode to test yourself! Compare numbers across systems using <, >, or =.
Try This
Explore: Tap "1 Lakh" preset. See how it's 1,00,000 in Indian but 100,000 in International — same number, different commas!
Explore: Tap "1 Crore" preset. In Indian it's 1,00,00,000 (1 followed by 7 zeros). In International it's 10,000,000 (Ten Million).
Quiz: Is 500 Lakhs bigger or smaller than 5 Million? Think carefully — 1 Lakh = 1,00,000, so 500 Lakhs = 5,00,00,000. But 5 Million = 50,00,000. So 500 Lakhs > 5 Million!
Key Concept
Indian system commas: 3-2-2-2 from right (Thousands → Lakhs → Crores → Arabs).
International system commas: 3-3-3 from right (Thousands → Millions → Billions).

Quick conversions: 1 Lakh = 100 Thousand | 10 Lakhs = 1 Million | 1 Crore = 10 Million | 1 Arab = 1 Billion
