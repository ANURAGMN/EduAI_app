Are They Equal?

Compare two expressions — plug in values and see!

Looks Similar ≠ Equal!
5u (5 times u) and 5 + u (5 plus u) look similar but give different values for almost every u. 10y − 3 and 10(y − 3) also look related but are fundamentally different operations. Always test with numbers to check!

How to Use

What To Do
1Two expressions are shown side by side. Both use the same variable.
2The table shows both expressions evaluated for 4 different values of the variable.
3Green rows mean both expressions gave the same value. Red rows mean they're different.
4If ANY row is red, the expressions are NOT equal in general.
Key Concept
Two expressions are equal only if they give the same value for EVERY possible number. Even one mismatch proves they're different!
