Words → Algebra

Translate situations into algebraic expressions

From Words to Symbols
"5 more than a number" → n + 5. "2 less than 13 times a number" → 13n − 2. "Perimeter of a regular hexagon with side a" → 6a. Algebra is just a shorter way to write what you already understand in words!

How to Use

What To Do
1Read the situation described in words.
2Pick the correct algebraic expression that matches it.
3Think about: what's the variable (unknown)? What operation is described?
Key Concept
"More than" → addition. "Less than" → subtraction. "Times" → multiplication. "Each" or "per" → multiplication. "Total" → addition of parts.
