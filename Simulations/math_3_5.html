Compare & Order Decimals

Which is bigger? Compare digit by digit!

The Zero Dilemma
Trailing zeros don't change a decimal: 0.2 = 0.20 = 0.200.
But zeros after the decimal point do: 0.2 (2 tenths) ≠ 0.02 (2 hundredths) ≠ 0.002 (2 thousandths).
More digits does NOT mean bigger: 0.9 > 0.123456!

How to Use

What To Do
1Compare: Two decimals side by side — tap <, =, or >.
2Closest: Find which decimal is nearest to a target number.
3Compare digit by digit from left. The first digit that differs decides!
Key Concept
To compare: align decimal points, pad with zeros, then compare left to right. 6.456 vs 6.465: units same (6), tenths same (4), hundredths: 5 vs 6 → 6.465 wins!
