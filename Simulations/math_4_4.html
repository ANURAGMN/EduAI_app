Find formulas hiding in patterns!

The Power of Algebra
Patterns in numbers can be described by algebraic formulas. Once you find the formula, you can predict ANY term — the 100th, the 1000th — instantly, without counting one by one!

How to Use

What To Do
1Matchsticks: See how triangle patterns grow. The formula 2y+1 predicts matchsticks at any step.
2Calendar: Discover why 2×2 square diagonal sums are always equal (2a+8).
3Quiz: Find formulas from patterns and predict values.
Key Concept
Look for: what stays the same (constant) and what changes (variable) as you go through the pattern. The relationship between position and value IS the formula.
